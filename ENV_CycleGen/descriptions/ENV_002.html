Damp Heat Cycle 2 – Frost Test

Reference duration: 240 h. Source: 2.ENV_Test_Plan_Cp_r00v05(1).docx.

Generator Interpretation and User Overrides

  • Only the current Word plan is used. The current condition table gives Modes 3.1 / 3.2. Ten cycles total 240 h; the first five contain frost.
  • Phase times remain an editable nominal interpretation of Figures 36/37. They are NOT claimed to be independently verified IEC setpoints. No timing changes from external standards have been imported.
  • Two warm rises of 90 min, two hot holds of 240 min and two falls of 90 min, with a 60-min intermediate room hold, form the warm portion. Frost tail: 150/30/180/90/90 min; non-frost tail: 540 min.
  • Each B/F function check is assumed to last 1 min at the start of the hot dwell. RH during rise/hot hold is 93%, falling RH is 80%. Unspecified room RH uses the general 60% default; the explicitly uncontrolled frost interval remains blank.

Test Procedure

Place the device under test (DUT) inside the test chamber, ensuring specimen spacing ≥10 cm, and distance from the temperature chamber ≥10 cm; assemble the test plan according to the test arrangement.

Activate the Constant Temperature and Humidity Test Chamber and set the parameters for the first five cycles as follows: starting temperature at 25℃, maximum temperature at 65℃, minimum temperature at -10℃; humidity maintained at (93±3)%RH during the 25℃ to 65℃ Temperature Rise Interval, and at 80%RH during the 65℃ to 25℃ Cooling Phase. Humidity is not controlled within the -10℃ to 25℃ temperature interval.

When the cycle temperature reaches the maximum of 65℃, switch the DUT to Operating Mode 3.2 for functional testing, conducting one functional test each during Interval B and Interval F as shown in the figure above. Each cycle lasts 24 hours, with a total of five cycles performed.

Parameters for the last 5 cycles: high temperature 65℃, low temperature 25℃, humidity (93±3)% RH during the 25℃ to 65℃ temperature rise interval; Humidity 80% RH during the 65℃ to 25℃ cooling phase;

When the cycle temperature reaches the maximum (65℃), the DUT shall be switched to Operating Mode 3.2 for functional testing, performing functional tests once each in Interval B and Interval F as illustrated above; each cycle lasts 24 hours, performed over 5 cycles;

Upon test completion, allow the specimen to naturally recover to room temperature, and then conduct a function inspection.

Test Class and Acceptance Criteria

After the testing sequence, a visual inspection of the product connector and internal components shall be performed. Metal components, PCB, connectors, and plastics shall be free of corrosion.

At the same temperature without humidity load, the current consumption deviation from the DUT typical value shall not exceed ±5%.

The functional status shall comply with Function Grade Class A.

Test Procedure

Place the device under test (DUT) inside the test chamber, ensuring specimen spacing ≥10 cm, and distance from the temperature chamber ≥10 cm; assemble the test plan according to the test arrangement.

Set the temperature chamber to Tmaxop. Once the test chamber temperature stabilizes, operate the specimen in the 3.2 operating mode for 47 hours;

After the second step, set the operating mode to 3.1 for 1 hour;

Upon test completion, allow the specimen to naturally recover to room temperature, and then conduct a function inspection.

Test Class and Acceptance Criteria

The test specimen must comply with all functions defined by the specification before, during, and after testing. Verification shall be conducted through continuous monitoring of all parameters throughout the test. Intermediate measurements during the endurance test are only permitted at 25%, 50%, and 75% completion if continuous measurement cannot be ensured throughout the part testing process.

Data acquired from continuous monitoring of component parameters must be analyzed for critical points, trends, and irregularities.

After testing, two samples shall be randomly selected for cover-opening inspection; refer to the requirements specified in section 4.

After testing, the specimen must satisfy visual inspection criteria; the appearance shall exhibit no significant damage, and no abnormal noise shall be heard from components after shaking.

Following testing, two samples shall be extracted for IRCL testing, with specific test locations to be confirmed by Xiaomi engineers.

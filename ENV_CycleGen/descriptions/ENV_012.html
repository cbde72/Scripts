Steady-state Damp Heat Test

Reference duration: 504 h. Source: 2.ENV_Test_Plan_Cp_r00v05(1).docx.

Generator Interpretation and User Overrides

  • The CURRENT conditions and procedure specify 504 h (21 days), at 65 °C / 93% RH. This supersedes the old 700-hour recipe.
  • Alternate 47 h Mode 2.1 and 1 h Mode 3.2; ensure the final hour is Mode 3.2. After ten complete 48-hour blocks the last partial block is 23 h inactive + 1 h active.
  • The explicit damp-heat RH setpoint is retained. The general 60% default does not replace specified humidity values.

Test Procedure

Place the device under test (DUT) inside the test chamber, ensuring specimen spacing ≥10 cm, and distance from the temperature chamber ≥10 cm; assemble the test plan according to the test arrangement.

Activate the constant temperature and humidity test chamber and set parameters as follows: temperature at 65±2℃, relative humidity at (93±3) %RH. Alternate between the 47-hour 2.1 operating mode and the 1-hour 3.2 operating mode, switching to the 3.2 operating mode during the final hour; the total test duration is 504 hours.

Upon test completion, allow the specimen to naturally recover to room temperature, and then conduct a function inspection.

Test Class and Acceptance Criteria

The DUT is required to meet Class A functional status before, during, and after the test.

At standard atmospheric pressure, after recovery, samples should not exhibit visible damage such as cracking, distortion, swelling, or softening.

Upon completion of the test sequence, two samples shall be selected for lid opening and visual inspection; no corrosion or other abnormalities shall be present inside the samples.
